Privacy policy

Aterra Finance Automation · last updated 11 October 2026

Who we are

Aterra is a small e-commerce business based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, selling on bol.com. This application is an internal tool operated by Aterra for its own bookkeeping. It has no external users. Contact: aterra.eu@gmail.com.

What the application does

The application connects to Google accounts owned by Aterra and its team members in order to:

  • store supplier invoices and related documents in Aterra's own Google Drive folders;
  • record invoice amounts in a Google Sheet owned by Aterra, used as a purchase ledger.

Google user data we access

With the account owner's consent, the application uses the Google Drive and Google Sheets APIs to create files in designated Aterra folders and to append rows to Aterra's ledger spreadsheet. It reads folder contents only to avoid creating duplicates. It does not access files outside the folders designated by Aterra, does not read other users' data, and does not access Gmail through Google APIs.

How the data is used

Data is used solely for Aterra's internal bookkeeping and cost tracking. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to Aterra's own administration.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred or disclosed to third parties. Documents and ledger entries remain in Aterra's Google Drive and are visible only to Aterra team members with whom Aterra has shared them. Aterra may share its own bookkeeping records with its accountant as required by Dutch law.

Storage and retention

Documents and ledger rows are stored in Aterra's Google Drive and Google Sheets and retained for as long as Dutch bookkeeping law requires (currently seven years). Access tokens are stored on Aterra's own computers only and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited use

Aterra's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights and contact

Account owners can withdraw consent at any time via their Google account permissions. Questions about this policy: aterra.eu@gmail.com.
