Scope

Aterra Finance Automation is an internal bookkeeping tool operated by Aterra (Amsterdam, the Netherlands) for its own use. It is not a public service and is not offered to third parties. Only Aterra team members may connect their Google accounts to it.

Use of the tool

The tool files supplier invoices into Aterra's Google Drive and records their amounts in Aterra's ledger spreadsheet. Users must only connect Google accounts that they control and that Aterra has authorised for this purpose.

Data

How Google user data is handled is described in the privacy policy. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Availability and liability

The tool is provided as is, for Aterra's internal administration, without any warranty. Aterra may change or stop it at any time. Bookkeeping records remain Aterra's responsibility and are reviewed by Aterra before use in its accounts.
