Aterra Finance Automation

Internal bookkeeping tool of Aterra (Amsterdam, the Netherlands), seller on bol.com.

This application is used only by Aterra's own team. It files supplier invoices into Aterra's Google Drive and records their amounts in a Google Sheet, so that purchase costs can be tracked per shipment and product.

It is not offered to the public and does not process data of customers or third parties.
